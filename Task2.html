We Help You Store Files Locally

AetherFS links regular computer units to store sharded data safely together. No single entity tracks your folders, keeping them completely private.

Get Started

How it works

Instead of sending your files to a massive company server, our experimental system handles files locally.

Smart File Splitting

Your files are cut into small pieces and safely mixed across multiple computers on the network so nobody can read your files but you.

Quick Indexing

The page tracks paths across active computers quickly, loading your personal data folders back together in just a few seconds.

Automatic Backups

If one computer on the network turns off, the remaining active systems instantly share and recreate any missing pieces automatically.
